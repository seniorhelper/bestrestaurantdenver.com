About This Guide

An independent Denver restaurant guide, built from the MICHELIN Guide Colorado.

Best Restaurant Denver is an independent guide to the best restaurants in Denver, Colorado. It is published by Eye To Ad Media, the Denver company founded by Zach Wennstedt, under Search Converts LLC, and it is edited by Zach Wennstedt.

What we do

Most "best restaurants in Denver" lists are someone's opinion presented as a ranking. Ours starts from an independent selection instead: the MICHELIN Guide Colorado, whose anonymous inspectors decide which Denver restaurants are recognized. We take that list and make it useful, searchable by name, neighborhood and cuisine, with addresses, links to official websites, and guides that explain each neighborhood and cuisine in context.

Where we add our own judgement, for example in the order of picks in a guide, how to get a reservation, or which neighborhoods make a good evening, we say so and explain our criteria.

What we do not do

  • We do not accept payment, free meals or gifts to list or feature a restaurant.
  • We do not publish star ratings, scores or reviews of our own, and we do not claim visits we did not make.
  • We do not publish hours, prices or phone numbers, which change too often to keep accurate. We link to each restaurant's own website where we could verify one.

How the site is funded

Best Restaurant Denver carries display advertising served by Google AdSense, kept separate from the editorial content. Advertisers have no influence over which restaurants appear or what we write about them. Our privacy policy explains advertising cookies.

Sister sites

For the rest of Colorado, including the mountain towns and Colorado Springs, see Best Restaurant Colorado. For things to do in and around the city, see Colorado What's Up and Denver Things To Do.
