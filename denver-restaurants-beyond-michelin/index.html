Denver Beyond MICHELIN

14 James Beard-recognized kitchens, cocktail bars and city institutions the 2026 guide does not list, each with its sources.Updated 10 October 2026 · Zach Wennstedt

Which great Denver restaurants does the MICHELIN Guide leave out?

Yuan Wonton, home of the 2026 James Beard Best Chef: Mountain, tops the list, followed by El Taco de Mexico, a James Beard America's Classic. Sắp Sửa, Molotov Kitschen + Cocktails, Lucina, Coperta, Le French and Spuntino are recent James Beard semifinalists. All 14 are below with their sources.

The MICHELIN Guide is a useful filter, but it is one filter. It listed 37 Denver restaurants in 2026, and the city's best-known chef win of the year went to a dumpling kitchen it does not list.

This page collects the Denver kitchens and bars that an independent body other than MICHELIN has recognized, plus two institutions with decades of press behind them. It follows the same rule as our directory: we do not add restaurants on our own say-so. Every entry has at least two published sources, which you can open under each listing, and the date we last checked it.

How this list is ordered

The James Beard winner and the America's Classics honoree come first, then semifinalists from most recent nomination back, then two long-running institutions, then the bars. It is a reading order, not a ranking.

James Beard recognition

Kitchens the James Beard Foundation noticed

The James Beard Awards run a national semifinalist list each January and a Mountain region category that covers Colorado, Idaho, Montana, Utah and Wyoming. Being named a semifinalist is a real distinction: the list is drawn from a large pool of public recommendations and then narrowed by a volunteer panel. Our sister site's explainer covers how the process works.

Denver institutions

Two places that are part of the city's story

Method

How we check a listing

  • Two sources minimum. At least two independent publications, or one publication plus the awarding body, describe the restaurant at its current address.
  • No closure reports. We search for closure or relocation news before adding and again at each review. Super Mega Bien, for example, announced its closure for March 2026, so it is not here.
  • No invented details. We do not publish ratings, review counts, prices or opening hours, and we do not describe dishes we have not seen in a source.
  • Right city. Restaurants in Aurora, Boulder or Edgewater belong on our statewide sister site, Best Restaurant Colorado.

All of this is also in our open data file, with every source link and the verification date for each record.

Common questions

Questions, Answered

What are the best Denver restaurants not in the MICHELIN Guide?

Start with Yuan Wonton, whose chef Penelope Wong won the 2026 James Beard Award for Best Chef: Mountain, and El Taco de Mexico, the 2020 James Beard America’s Classics honoree on Santa Fe Drive. Sắp Sửa, Molotov Kitschen + Cocktails, Lucina and Coperta have all had James Beard semifinalist nods since 2023.

Why are these restaurants not in the MICHELIN Guide?

MICHELIN does not explain omissions, and inspectors visit far more places than they list. Not being listed is not a verdict. These restaurants are here because a different independent body, the James Beard Foundation, or decades of press coverage point to them.

How did you choose who to include?

Each place needed at least two independent published sources, a current Denver street address and no report of closure when we checked on 10 October 2026. We did not include anywhere on the strength of our own opinion, and nobody paid to be listed.

Is Molino Chido in Denver?

No. Tommy Lee’s Mexico City-style taqueria opened in November 2025 at Stanley Marketplace, which is in Aurora. Our statewide sister site, Best Restaurant Colorado, lists it under Aurora.
