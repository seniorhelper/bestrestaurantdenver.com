Why Denver is worth showing up for

Denver isn’t just a locals’ market. Visit Denver counted 37.1 million domestic visitors in 2024, and the 19.8 million who stayed overnight spent $8.7 billion. Restaurant food and drink alone came to nearly $1.5 billion of visitor spending.

Most of those visitors pick restaurants on their phones, often the same afternoon. A listing with a photo, current hours and a menu they can read is what turns that search into a booked table.

Questions owners ask

Is a basic listing really free?

Yes. Restaurants in Denver can request an accurate basic listing on Best Restaurant Denver for free: name, area, cuisine, price range, hours and links. We verify you’re the owner or manager before changes go live. Featured and Menu Pro are optional upgrades, and nothing you buy changes whether we keep you listed.

What is the difference between Featured and Menu Pro?

Featured ($29 a month) gives you a top spot on Best Restaurant Denver with a badge, a photo, a pitch and your link. Menu Pro ($49 a month or $490 a year) includes the same Featured placement plus a hosted, Google-readable menu page, a full menu update every quarter and NFC table tags, all done for you.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No, and we won’t pretend it does. We promise the work: a clearly labeled top placement, your photo and link, and with Menu Pro a menu that search engines can read and that we keep current. How many diners choose you still depends on your food, reviews and service.

How fast does it go live?

Usually within 48 hours of getting your photo, pitch and link. Menu Pro menu pages go live within 48 hours of receiving your menu. We confirm you run the restaurant first, so a quick reply to our welcome email keeps things moving.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime online and stay live until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Menu Pro yearly is one payment of $490 for twelve months, which is two months free compared with paying monthly.

Does being Featured affect the MICHELIN listings in this guide?

No. The MICHELIN Guide Colorado selections we report come from MICHELIN, and our editorial lists are ours. Featured spots are separate, labeled placements on the guide pages that match your neighborhood or cuisine. Paying never adds or removes a MICHELIN distinction, and it never moves you inside an editorial ranking.
