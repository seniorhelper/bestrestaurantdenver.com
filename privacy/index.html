Privacy Policy

What we collect, how advertising cookies work and your choices.

Last updated 10 October 2026. This policy explains what information Best Restaurant Denver (bestrestaurantdenver.com) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Best Restaurant Denver is an independent restaurant guide published by Eye To Ad Media in Denver, Colorado.

Information you give us

If you use our contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you type, plus the inquiry type you select. The form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards the message to our inbox. We use what you send only to read and answer your message and to correct the guide when you point out an error. We do not sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when a page is requested, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These server logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google may ask for your consent before personalized ads or ad-related cookies are used, through a consent message that meets Google's EU user consent policy. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time through the same consent message or your browser settings. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use cookies only for purposes such as frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies will not stop you from reading anything on this site.

Fonts and embedded services

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests a font file, Google receives your IP address as part of that request. We do not embed social media trackers or analytics pixels beyond the advertising services described above.

Links to other websites

We link to restaurants, award bodies, tourism offices and news outlets so you can check our facts. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children

This site is written for adults planning meals and trips. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us to delete any message you sent through the contact form. To do that, or to ask anything about this policy, use the contact page or email info@eyetoad.com. If we change this policy, the date at the top of this page will change.
