South Pearl, Wash Park
& Cherry Creek

Two Michelin stars in one South Pearl building, Italian rooms around Washington Park, Cherry Creek's dining rooms and Baker's Bib Gourmand.Updated 10 October 2026 · Best Restaurant Denver editors

What are the best restaurants in south and central Denver?

Platt Park's 1551 S. Pearl Street holds two one-star restaurants, Kizaki and Margot, in one building. Nearby, Washington Park has Restaurant Olivia and the new Florence Supper Club, Cherry Creek has Barolo Grill and Alteño, Baker has the Bib Gourmand MAKfam, and Madeline joined the guide in 2026 near Cheesman Park.

South of downtown, Denver's best restaurants are spread through residential neighborhoods rather than packed into one district. That makes them easier to book and harder to stumble upon.

This guide covers the quieter half of the city's Michelin list: eight restaurants across South Pearl Street, Washington Park, Cherry Creek, South Broadway and East Sixth Avenue. None is more than about fifteen minutes by car from downtown, and several are within a short walk of each other on South Pearl.

How we picked these

We start with South Pearl Street, where the stars are, then move to Washington Park, Cherry Creek, Baker and Cheesman Park. Within each area, higher Michelin distinctions come first. We publish no ratings of our own, and inclusion cannot be bought.

South Pearl Street

Two stars, one building

In 2025 a single building at 1551 South Pearl Street in Platt Park produced two Michelin stars. Both kept them in 2026.

  1. One Michelin Star

    Kizaki

    Japanese · 1551 S. Pearl St.

    Toshi Kizaki co-founded Sushi Den, one of Denver's best-known sushi restaurants, and returned to the counter with this Edomae-style omakase inside the Denchu space. Westword described a nine-seat chef's counter plus two booths. It earned a first star in 2025 and was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Best New Restaurant.

    Official website

  2. One Michelin Star

    Margot

    Tasting menu · 1551 S. Pearl St.

    Chef Justin Fulton ran pop-ups for three years before opening Margot in June 2025; it was starred within months. Michelin describes an eight-seat counter for a seasonal multicourse menu inside a larger shared space, and the bar and dining room serve à la carte, which is the easier way in.

    MICHELIN Guide listing

A few blocks north on South Pearl, Florence Supper Club at 375 S. Pearl Street joined the recommended list in 2026 with a classic Italian-American supper-club menu.

Washington Park and Cherry Creek

Pasta, Piedmont and a hotel taqueria

  1. Recommended

    Restaurant Olivia

    Italian · 290 S. Downing St.

    A pasta-focused restaurant on South Downing Street, from chef Ty Leon, beverage director Austin Carson and hospitality director Heather Morrison. Morrison won Michelin's 2025 Outstanding Service Award, and Olivia was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Outstanding Hospitality.

    Official website

  2. Recommended New 2026

    Florence Supper Club

    Italian · 375 S. Pearl St.

    Italian-American cooking in the red-sauce tradition at the north end of South Pearl, new to the guide in 2026.

    MICHELIN Guide listing

  3. Recommended

    Barolo Grill

    Italian · 3030 E. Sixth Ave.

    A long-running Italian dining room on East Sixth Avenue at the edge of Cherry Creek. Ryan Fletter was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Outstanding Professional in Beverage Service, so the wine list is the point.

    Official website

  4. Recommended

    Alteño

    Mexican · 249 Clayton St.

    Johnny Curiel's Mexican restaurant on the ground floor of the Clayton Hotel and Members Club, new to the guide in 2025. A good option in Cherry Creek when the LoHi restaurants are booked.

    MICHELIN Guide listing

Baker and Cheesman Park

A counter on Broadway, a newcomer on Sixth

  1. Bib Gourmand

    MAKfam

    Chinese · 39 W. First Ave.

    Chef Kenneth Wan and Doris Yuen ran pop-ups in New York and New Jersey, moved to Denver in 2019, cooked from a food-hall stall at Avanti, then opened this counter-service Chinese restaurant on West First Avenue in Baker. It holds a Bib Gourmand, Wan was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Best Chef: Mountain, and a larger second location has been announced for RiNo. Most tables are for walk-ins.

    MICHELIN Guide listing

  2. Recommended New 2026

    Madeline

    Contemporary · 1313 E. Sixth Ave.

    Quincy Cherrett's seasonal New American restaurant opened in May 2026 in the small former Fruition space on East Sixth Avenue, south of Cheesman Park, and joined the guide four months later.

    MICHELIN Guide listing

Planning a southside evening

  • South Pearl on foot. Kizaki, Margot and Florence Supper Club are on the same street; park once and walk.
  • Counters book first. Kizaki and Margot seat very few people; see our tasting menu guide.
  • Cherry Creek is easier. Barolo Grill and Alteño usually have more flexibility, and parking is simpler than in RiNo or LoHi.
  • MAKfam is a walk-in. Arrive early on weekends.

Common questions

Questions, Answered

Which South Pearl Street restaurants have MICHELIN stars?

Kizaki and Margot, both at 1551 S. Pearl Street in Platt Park, each hold one Michelin star. Florence Supper Club, at 375 S. Pearl Street, joined the recommended list in 2026.

Are there MICHELIN restaurants in Cherry Creek?

Yes. Barolo Grill and Alteño are both recommended in the 2026 MICHELIN Guide Colorado.

Does MAKfam take reservations?

Mostly not. Coverage of MAKfam describes it as counter service with most tables kept for walk-ins and limited reservations, so check its current policy before you go.
